## Direction contract

THESIS: JuneGiri is the carved pahari house you sleep in, not a resort brochure. The page is built from Garhwali woodcraft — the hand-carved window frame (khyoli), the indigo-painted shutter, the slate roof, mud-ochre plaster — so trust comes from craft and place, not from stock gloss. It refuses the category default: the full-bleed photo hero under a clean sans with a cyan accent, and it refuses the kicker-eyebrow entirely.

OWN-WORLD: Ground is warm mud-plaster oat (NOT cream), alt sections in deeper plaster and in Garhwali door-indigo panels. Walnut ink for type. Terracotta/clay is the primary action; brass-marigold lights stars and highlights; a ringaal moss-green secondary. Signature material: a carved double-keyline frame with corner notches (the khyoli) around hero lockup, room/experience cards and the booking panel; indigo shutter-panels as section breaks; subtle plaster grain. Display type is Marcellus (architectural carved-Roman caps); body/UI is Hind (humanist, Devanagari-rooted). Drawn SVG icons in one stroke weight — never emoji.

STORY: A metro family lands, immediately reads "a real family's carved pahari home at the forest edge," sees honest proof (5.0★, real guests, the hosts, real photos), and ends in a one-tap WhatsApp conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed real forest-edge photography behind a warm indigo/walnut wash, held inside a carved khyoli frame. Left lockup: Marcellus H1 ("A carved pahari home at the edge of Rajaji forest") with a short Hind lead and four ledger-style trust facts on a carved rule. Primary action = terracotta "Check availability", secondary = WhatsApp, sitting in / just below a carved wooden booking panel that overlaps the hero's lower edge. No eyebrow.

FORM: Garhwali carved-wood vernacular (khyoli/shutter/slate/plaster). Position #1 on my grounded list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), user-pinned over the dice-assigned #6. Raises folded from declined challengers: single-seed token coherence (one wood-and-indigo grammar to every atom), staged threshold arrival (gate → courtyard → rooms as the scroll), addressable card-with-trail, and real mechanical motion (a shutter-open reveal + latch-press buttons, with weight — not generic fades). Seed key: c17ab705.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
